About

Backend-focused, product-minded.

I'm a software engineer with 8 years of experience, most of it in PHP and Laravel, including 2 years of .NET Core. Since 2023 I've been building fintech systems: wallets and fund transfers, crypto and fiat payment integrations, a double-entry transaction ledger, and automated matching of deposits against gateway records, for a high-volume platform where every transaction must be recorded exactly once, across about 1 million transaction, ledger and bonus entries a day (case study).

Before fintech, I spent years on ERP, HR and payroll, and accounting systems. I led the team behind a multi-company ERP for two steel companies, engineered a central OAuth2 authentication server for the company's multi-tenant products, and built Navy Care, a telemedicine system for three Bangladesh Navy hospitals, as the sole developer.

Now I'm also technical lead on Massara, a real estate marketplace SaaS at KoldaTech. I use Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot and Cursor every day, and review and test every change by hand.
